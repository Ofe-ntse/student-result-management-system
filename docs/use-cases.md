# Use Cases
**1. Administrator**
**Login**
The administrator enters their username and password to access the system.

**Add Student**
The administrator enters the student's details and saves the new student record.

**Update Student**
The administrator selects a student and changes their information.

**Delete Student**
The administrator selects a student record and deletes it from the system.

**Search Student**
The administrator enters a student ID to find a student's information.

**Manage Subjects**
The administrator can manage the subjects stored in the system.

**Manage Results**
The administrator can record and manage student academic information.

**2. Teacher**
**Login**
The teacher logs into the system using their login details.

**Capture Marks**
The teacher enters marks for a student's assessments.

**Update Marks**
The teacher can change marks if an incorrect mark was entered.

**View Performance**
The teacher can view student results and performance information.

**3. Student**
**Login**
The student logs into the system.

**View Personal Information**
The student can view their own information.

**View Subjects**
The student can view the subjects they are registered for.

**View Results**
The student can view their academic results.

**View Total and Average**
The student can view their total and average marks.

**View Pass or Fail Status**
The student can see whether they have passed or failed.
The student does not have permission to change their academic records.

Main Actors


*Administrator | Manage students, subjects and system information 

*Teacher       | Enter, update and view student marks   

*Student       | View personal information, subjects and results
